# ORIGINS: where Asmodensius's ideas come from

**Policy (Alex, 2026-10-31).** All code, designs and distinctive mechanics are 100% original. Nothing is reused from other A-Life or neuro-simulation projects, even under MIT or Apache licences. Ideas come only from published science and are cited here.

**Allowed third-party:**
- Alex's/Tsotchke's own Eshkol, Moonlab and quantum_rng.
- Neuralese, Alex's own project.
- Standard libraries: language runtimes, React, three.js (approved by Alex, 2026-10-31).

**Enforced by** `lab/conformance/originality.ts`, a required check run from `lab/conformance/run.ts` (`out/originality.json`).

## Concepts and their published sources (v4.0)
- **Predictive allostasis / interoception:** Sterling 2012, *Physiol Behav* 106:5; Barrett & Simmons 2015, *Nat Rev Neurosci* 16:419.
- **Uncertainty-driven exploration; ACh/NE as expected/unexpected uncertainty:** Yu & Dayan 2005, *Neuron* 46:681; Gershman 2018, *Cognition* 173:34.
- **Dopamine reward prediction error:** Schultz, Dayan & Montague 1997, *Science* 275:1593.
- **Global workspace:** Baars 1988; Dehaene & Changeux 2011, *Neuron* 70:200.
- **Sleep replay and synaptic homeostasis:** Wilson & McNaughton 1994, *Science* 265:676; Tononi & Cirelli 2014, *Neuron* 81:12.
- **Two-process sleep model:** Borbély 1982, *Hum Neurobiol* 1:195.
- **Emotions as functional states / core affect (valence and arousal):** Russell 2003, *Psychol Rev* 110:145; Adolphs & Anderson 2018, *The Neuroscience of Emotion*.
- **Mood as a slow expectation-of-reward signal:** Eldar et al. 2016, *Trends Cogn Sci* 20:15.
- **Attitudes and learned valence:** Rescorla & Wagner 1972 (associative learning).
- **Personality in animals, heritability, lifetime change:** Réale et al. 2007, *Biol Rev* 82:291; Dingemanse & Wolf 2010, *Phil Trans B* 365:3947.
- **HPA axis, allostatic load, glucocorticoid feedback failure:** McEwen 1998, *NEJM* 338:171; Sapolsky, Krey & McEwen 1986, *Endocr Rev* 7:284.
- **Incentive sensitisation (wanting vs liking), tolerance:** Robinson & Berridge 1993, *Brain Res Rev* 18:247.
- **Sickness behaviour:** Hart 1988, *Neurosci Biobehav Rev* 12:123; Dantzer et al. 2008, *Nat Rev Neurosci* 9:46.
- **Rumination / mood instability:** Nolen-Hoeksema 1991, *J Abnorm Psychol* 100:569.
- **Bet-hedging:** Slatkin 1974, *Evolution* 28:157; Philippi & Seger 1989, *TREE* 4:41.
- **Risk-sensitive foraging (energy-budget rule):** Caraco, Martindale & Whittam 1980, *Anim Behav* 28:820; Stephens 1981, *Anim Behav* 29:628.
- **Activation patching / causal intervention on internal state:** Vig et al. 2020 (NeurIPS); Meng et al. 2022 (NeurIPS).
- **Born-rule measurement as a noise source:** standard quantum mechanics; generated by Alex's Moonlab/quantum_rng.
- **Developmental wiring:** chemoaffinity, Sperry 1963, *PNAS* 50:703; topographic guidance gradients, Goodhill & Richards 1999, *TINS* 22:529; overproduction then competitive elimination, Purves & Lichtman 1980, *Science* 210:153; synaptic density peaks then declines, Huttenlocher 1979, *Brain Res* 163:195.
- **Sleep-dependent pruning and activity-dependent refinement:** Tononi & Cirelli 2014, *Neuron* 81:12; Li, Ma, Yang & Gan 2017, *Nat Neurosci* 20:427; Katz & Shatz 1996, *Science* 274:1133.

- Knowledge gradient / value of information (v4.0 kgK option): Howard RA (1966) Information value theory, IEEE Trans Syst Sci Cybern 2:22-26; Frazier PI, Powell WB, Dayanik S (2008) A knowledge-gradient policy for sequential information collection, SIAM J Control Optim 47:2410-2439.
- Veto of prepared action ("free won't", v4.0 N13): Brass M, Haggard P (2007) To do or not to do: the neural signature of self-control, J Neurosci 27:9141-9145.
