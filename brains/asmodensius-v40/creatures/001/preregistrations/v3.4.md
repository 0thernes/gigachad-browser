# Preregistration v3.4: a developing, balanced, plastic cortex (and LTC with learned gates) under the full v3.1 + v3.3 suites

Status: **PLAN, committed before any v3.4 code was written** (2026-10-29, Round 15). A §F frozen addendum will be appended after tuning on seeds 2000..2015. It will be committed before the first run on test seeds 1000..1015, and every deviation from this plan will be listed there. v1..v3.3 results stay on record unchanged. That includes the v3.0 C2/C8b failures, the v3.1 classification failures, the v3.2 C9 regression, the v3.3 LTC null result and the CAP-C reversed-direction failure.

## 0. Diagnosis that motivates v3.4 (measured on tuning seeds 2000..2002 only, before writing this plan)
- **Immediate decodability.** A nearest-centroid probe on module-0 rates on the stimulus tick decodes a 1-of-4 stimulus at 0.88–0.92. So the v3.1 cortex already carries the stimulus at the tick it arrives. The earlier one-line summary "the cortex does not carry a decodable stimulus" was only half right.
- **What actually fails (a): slow readout learning.** The Mind's own online classifier (delta rule on raw rates) reaches only 0.48–0.68 after 100 trials. The rates are dense (≈0.35–0.67 spikes/substep for every neuron) and sit on a large common offset.
- **What actually fails (b): no persistence.** One or two blank ticks later, both the probe and the Mind classifier are at chance (0.20–0.37). The cortex keeps no trace, and that sinks RPT-1, delayed or broadcast readouts, and everything downstream.

## A. Mechanisms to add inside organism.ts (option `cortex: "v34"`, the new default; `cortex: "v31"` reproduces the v3.1 cortex bit-for-bit)
1. **Topographic thalamocortical projection.** THAL line (m, j) projects to every excitatory neuron n of CTX module m with a Gaussian weight over the neuron's preferred position (receptive field), plus seeded jitter. A separate RNG stream is used, so the v3.0 random wiring is unchanged. Feedforward inhibition runs THAL → module interneurons.
2. **E/I balance.** Each module gets 3 of the 12 cortical interneurons. The loops are E → I (recurrent feedback inhibition) and I → E (lateral, divisive-like). Inhibitory synapses learn with the Vogels et al. (2011) rule toward a target excitatory rate.
3. **STDP plus homeostasis.** Thalamocortical and within-module recurrent synapses use pair-based STDP with pre/post traces at substep resolution, gated by ACh/DA. Each neuron's incoming thalamocortical weights are normalised to a fixed sum (competition). Each excitatory neuron's threshold adapts slowly toward a target rate (intrinsic homeostatic plasticity). The existing N3 synaptic down-scaling is kept.
4. **Recurrent persistence.** Within-module recurrent excitation carries a slow NMDA-like synaptic current (decay on the order of ticks). Hebbian STDP forms stimulus-specific assemblies during development. Inhibition makes the module winner-take-most, so one assembly can reverberate across blank ticks.
5. **Developmental phase.** For ticks 0..479 the organism receives spontaneous, patterned sensory waves, independent per modality and not gated by sleep (cf. retinal and cochlear waves before sensory onset). A critical-period plasticity multiplier decays to the adult level at tick 480, which is when every v3.1 task starts.
6. **Readout normalisation.** Mind readouts see rates minus a slow per-neuron running mean (adaptation). This applies to the classifier, value and predictor inputs.

The workspace, recurrence, metacognition and prediction mechanisms are unchanged in form. Their constants may be re-tuned on seeds 2000..2015 so that they are *measurably useful* (their lesion controls lose) once the cortex encodes and holds stimuli.

## B. Learned-gate LTC (option `ltc: "learned"`)
- **Gates.** Each population (CTX-E, CTX-I, HIP, THAL) gets gate parameters θ = (log τ0, a_ACh, b_in, c_NE) in the same τ formula as v3.3.
- **Learning rule.** For CTX-E, θ is learned by an e-prop rule: an online, local approximation to BPTT for spiking networks (Bellec et al., Nat. Commun. 11:3625, 2020). Each neuron keeps an eligibility trace ∂v/∂θ through its leaky dynamics, multiplied by a spike pseudo-derivative. The learning signal is the Mind readout error fed back through the readout weights (symmetric e-prop). This follows Yin, Corradi & Bohté 2023, whose LTC-SN time constants are trained by gradient (FPTT/BPTT).
- **Training.** The rule runs on tuning seeds 2000..2015 over a fixed curriculum of immediate and delayed classification plus sensory prediction. The population-mean θ across tuning seeds is frozen into organism.ts as a constant and recorded in §F. On test seeds the learned gates are used as frozen constants. Populations without a learning signal keep the v3.3 hand-set gates; this is reported as a limitation.

## C. Items (test seeds 1000..1015, 16 per arm; pass rule d ≥ 0.8 and t ≥ 2.75 vs every control; v3.1 non-ceiling rule for bounded metrics)
1. **The full v3.1 suite** (Butlin-14, 10 frameworks, CQM-25), same protocols, metrics and controls as v3.1, run on the v3.4 organism. Rollups are reported as Butlin x/14, frameworks x/10 and CQM x/25. The aliases are 13 = HOT-2, 19 = RPT-1, 20 = GWT-3, 21 = iit4. CQM23 is not applicable (counted as a fail). CQM24/25 are implementation checks.
2. **The full v3.3 suite** (L1..L5), run on the v3.4 organism in two comparisons:
   - (a) learned-gate LTC vs fixed τ (primary);
   - (b) hand-set LTC vs fixed τ (v3.3 replication on the new cortex).
3. **Cortex items K1a..K1e.** The metric is the accuracy of the Mind's own module-0 classifier (`post[0]`) on trials 100..159 of an immediate 1-of-4 classification protocol (the RPT-1 protocol at zero delay). The full v3.4 organism is compared against:
   - K1a: the v3.1 cortex (`cortex: "v31"`)
   - K1b: no developmental waves (`devLesion`)
   - K1c: no STDP (`stdpLesion`)
   - K1d: no homeostasis (intrinsic + inhibitory; `homeoLesion`)
   - K1e: interneuron loops lesioned (`eiLesion`)
   
   Each is a separate item, so the report shows which mechanisms are necessary. K1a..K1e are bounded, so the non-ceiling rule applies.
4. **K2 persistence.** Nearest-centroid probe accuracy 2 blank ticks after the stimulus (probe = test-owned measurement device). The control is the NMDA-like recurrent current lesioned (`nmdaLesion`).
5. **Regression, reported but not counted:** the v3.0 suite (C1..C11), run on the v3.4 code.

## E. Honesty constraints
- No sign flips, no aggregate consciousness score.
- Development, STDP and homeostasis are standard computational-neuroscience mechanisms adapted at toy scale (≈64 cortical neurons). A pass shows the mechanism is causally necessary for the metric inside this organism, nothing more.
- Learned LTC gates are learned by an approximate gradient (e-prop), not exact BPTT.

**Deviations from §A–E (all decided on tuning seeds only):**
1. Developmental waves are injected into THAL, not SENS. The organism sleeps for ticks 0–~440, and the sleep gate blocks SENS.
2. STDP is multiplicative (Δw ∝ w·trace) and gated by ACh only (the prereg said ACh/DA). Additive STDP washed out the TC map during sleep spindles.
3. Two-sided Sinkhorn TC normalisation: per-E incoming sum = tcSum, per-THAL outgoing sum = nO·tcSum/4. Vogels inhibitory weights are bounded to [−3·iE, 0]. Intrinsic thresholds are clamped to [−0.3, 2] and ACh-gated.
4. Global Glu/GABA release from CTX is scaled by gluK = 3.0 (v3.4 cortex only). Without it, the sparser cortex shifted Glu.P and HYP excitability and broke feeding (C10 0.63). 2.5 vs 3.0 was chosen on C10/C17 tuning (3.0: C10 0.98, C17 −0.35; 2.5: 0.94, −0.53). Consequence: C10 now fails the non-ceiling rule on tuning seeds. It is NOT re-tuned to dodge the ceiling.
5. Salience form gains a term `− ampBase·amp_prev` (onset salience). It is frozen at ampBase = 0, so it has no effect. Tried and rejected because it did not fix GWT-2.
6. The e-prop learning signal is classifier error plus cortical reconstruction error, not sensory prediction error. It applies to CTX-E gates only.

**Corrected diagnosis (tuning seeds):**
- The v3.4 cortex encodes stimuli: K1 post[0] accuracy is 0.976 vs 0.556 for the v3.1 cortex, and K2 two-tick persistence is 0.60 vs 0.27 with NMDA lesioned.
- Development is NOT necessary: devLesion gives 0.974, because the Gaussian TC initialisation is already topographic. E/I loops are not necessary for K1 either: the lesion scores 0.999.
- GWT-2 is still near chance after pretraining (module accuracy falls from 0.95 to ~0.78 with joint pretraining; workspace selection is near chance). Root cause unresolved: lesioning mixing, STDP, NMDA or top-down does not explain it, and readout gain does not fix it.

**Tuning-seed outcome (results/v34_tuning/summary_tune.txt):** Butlin 7/14, frameworks 6/10, CQM 16/25, K 1/6 (K1a–e fail the ceiling rule), LTC learned-vs-fixed 0/5 and hand-set-vs-fixed 0/5. L4 (both arms) is worse than fixed.
