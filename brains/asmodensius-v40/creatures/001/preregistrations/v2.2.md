# Preregistration v2.2: held-out AST-1R and 10 roadmap capability tests

Frozen 2026-10-28 (America/Los_Angeles). Committed before any v2.2 test code was written or run.
v1, v2 and v2.1 are unchanged, and their results stay reported as they are. The frozen v1 AST-1 result is reported as-is.

## Shared rules
- 16 seeds per condition (1000..1015 unless stated), mulberry32. Pass = Cohen's d ≥ 0.8 AND Welch t ≥ 2.75 against **every** listed control, using the frozen v1 `compare()`. Zero-variance ∞ is accepted, as in v2.1.
- "Additional requirement" lines must also hold.
- Tests, metrics, controls, inputs and thresholds are frozen. Only creature mechanisms may change, and every change is logged in HARNESS_CHANGES.md.
- A roadmap capability is marked **achieved** only if its test passes. The page must say these are small harness models of each capability, not demonstrations in the rendered creature, unless stated otherwise.

## AST-1R: attention schema on held-out seed sets (tail-robust)
Task: `attnRun` exactly as in v1 (non-social). Seed bases B ∈ {2000,3000,4000,5000,6000,7000,8000,9000}, seeds B..B+15, stream mulberry(s·7919+11).
Metric per seed: −log10(mean a² + 1e-6), where mean a² = −attnRun(...).
Controls (per base): noschema; randschema. All 16 comparisons must pass.
Justification: in held-out bases, drift beyond the actuator makes the squared error heavy-tailed, even for an oracle controller (results/heldout_ast_r7.txt). The log error is the standard variance-stabilizing transform for multiplicative error.

## RM-10 Interoceptive prediction of energy (roadmap 10)
World (400 steps): E₀=1, E_{t+1} = E_t − (0.02 + 0.01·sin(2πt/37 + φ)) + 0.3·F_t, with F_t ~ Bernoulli(0.05) and φ ~ U(0,2π) per seed.
The agent sees E_0..E_t and its own activity schedule (efference copy of the sin term). It forecasts E_{t+20}.
Metric: −MSE over t ∈ [50,380). Control: persistence forecast Ê = E_t.

## RM-27 Immune-like detection of corrupted weights (roadmap 27)
Weights w_i = hh(s·4096+i)/2³²·2−1 for i<4096. 64 random indices are replaced with U(−1,1). The detector scores every weight; higher means more suspicious.
Metric: AUROC (corrupted = positive). Control: random detector (U(0,1) scores). Additional requirement: mean AUROC ≥ 0.9.

## RM-28 Circadian entrainment (roadmap 28)
Light L_t = 1 if (t mod 24) < 12, else 0. The oscillator's intrinsic period is τ ~ U(22,26) per seed, with phase noise N(0,0.02²) per step. The light coupling is a creature mechanism.
Metric: PLV = |mean_t exp(i(θ_t − 2πt/24))| over t ∈ [480,960). Control: the same oscillator with light input removed.
Additional requirement: after light is removed at t=960, the full oscillator's phase relative to 2πt/24 changes by > 0.3 rad between t=960 and t=1200 in ≥ 15/16 seeds (free-run drift).

## RM-46 Reversible computing core (roadmap 46)
State: the 256 v2.1 inputs v21Inputs(s). Forward: 16 rounds of hh. Backward: 16 rounds of the creature's inverse map.
Metric: fraction of the 256 states restored bit-exactly. Control: backward replaced by 16 more forward rounds of hh.
Additional requirement: every full seed = 1.0.

## RM-47 Cryptographic lineage provenance (roadmap 47)
A lineage of 64 records. Genome₀ = 64 bytes from mulberry(s·31+7). Each child flips 1–3 random bits of its parent's genome. Record k stores genome_k and h_k = SHA-256(h_{k−1} ‖ genome_k), with h_{−1} = SHA-256("seed:"+s). The verifier holds only the head hash h_63.
Tamper trial (100 per seed): flip 1 random bit in a random record's genome. The adversary recomputes that record's own stored hash field.
Metric: fraction of trials detected. Control: unchained records with h_k = SHA-256(genome_k), each checked only against its own stored field.
Additional requirement: every full seed = 1.0.

## RM-61 Metacognitive confidence reporting (roadmap 61)
`firstOrder(r,400)` from v1 HOT-2. Metric: AUROC of the creature's confidence for correctness. Control: shuffled confidence.
Additional requirement: mean AUROC ≥ 0.7.

## RM-69 Serial dependence in perception (roadmap 69)
Stimuli (300 trials): with probability 0.7, s_t = clip(s_{t−1} + N(0,0.1²), −1, 1); otherwise s_t ~ U(−1,1). Observation o_t = s_t + N(0,0.5²). The perceiver outputs an estimate ŝ_t.
Metrics: (i) serial bias = OLS slope of (ŝ_t − s_t) on (s_{t−1} − s_t); (ii) −MSE(ŝ, s).
Control for both: memoryless perceiver ŝ_t = o_t.

## RM-79 Temporal interval timing (roadmap 79)
Target intervals I ∈ {10, 20, 40, 80}, 40 reproductions each. The creature's timer is a mechanism.
Metric: SD(reproduction | I=80) / SD(reproduction | I=10). Control: a timer with additive Gaussian noise N(0,2²) only, independent of I.
Additional requirement: the full mean ratio lies in [4, 16] (scalar property predicts 8).

## RM-80 Habituation and dishabituation (roadmap 80)
Stimulus A is presented 20 times, then a novel B once. Response noise N(0,0.05²).
Metrics: (i) decrement R(A₁) − R(A₂₀); (ii) recovery R(B) − R(A₂₀).
Control for both: a non-adaptive response unit with the same noise.

## RM-81 Classical conditioning with blocking (roadmap 81)
α ~ U(0.1,0.3) per seed. Reward λ = 1 with probability 0.9, else 0.
Blocking group: A+ ×30, then AB+ ×30. Control group: CD+ ×30 only.
Metric: blocking index = V_D(control group) − V_B(blocking group). Control: an associative-only learner (each cue updates on its own error, ΔV_i = α(λ − V_i)).

## Integrity
Any later change to these tests is a new preregistration (v2.3), and v2.2 results are kept.
