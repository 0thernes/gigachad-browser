## Fixed statistics (identical to v1)
16 seeds (seed k = 1000 + k), mulberry32. Each comparison passes iff Cohen's d (pooled SD, full better) >= 0.8
AND Welch t >= 2.75. An item passes iff every listed comparison passes. No aggregate score.
After this file is committed, the v2 environments, metrics, controls and thresholds are frozen. Only the creature's own
mechanisms (the "full" agent) may change, and every change and variant count is logged in HARNESS_CHANGES.md.
The test code lives in harness.ts under the `// === PREREG v2 TESTS (frozen) ===` banner. The agent code is outside that banner.
Agents receive only the observations listed below and never receive the seed, the test id, or the hidden state.

## V2-AI: active inference in a nonstationary, depleting foraging world (replaces the ceiling in v1 activeInference)
Why: in v1, the greedy passive policy was already optimal (viability 1.0, SD 0), so no agent could beat it. Active-inference
claims concern epistemic value under uncertainty and volatility (Friston et al. 2015, "Active inference and epistemic value";
Schwartenbeck et al. 2019). Volatile environments are the standard setting where belief updating and information seeking
matter (Behrens et al. 2007). Depleting patches follow the foraging literature (Charnov 1976, marginal value theorem).
Environment, per seed: 1 life of T = 300 steps. 4 sites. The food site is drawn from the prior p0 = [0.4, 0.3, 0.2, 0.1].
At every step, with hazard h = 0.12 the food relocates (it is redrawn from p0). Energy starts at 6 and is capped at 8.
Actions: look(i) costs 0.5 and returns o = [food at i], flipped with probability 0.1. go(i) costs 1.5; if food is at i the agent
gains +3.5 and the food is consumed (depleted) and redrawn from p0; otherwise it observes "empty". Once energy <= 0 the agent is
dead for the rest of the life.
Observations available to the agent: its energy, its own past actions and their outcomes, p0, h, the costs and the noise level.
Metrics: (a) viability = fraction of the 300 steps alive. (b) −mean over all 300 steps of −log2 b_t(food_t), where b_t is the
agent's reported belief over sites (dead agents keep a frozen belief).
Controls: passive = greedy go to the argmax of the same Bayesian, hazard-aware belief and never looks; random = uniform over the
8 actions with the same belief filter.
Comparisons: viability vs passive, viability vs random, belief accuracy vs passive, belief accuracy vs random.

## V2-10: autonomous goal regulation with drifting needs and depleting resources (replaces the ceiling in v1 CQM-10)
Why: homeostatic RL defines reward as drive reduction across multiple internal variables (Keramati & Gutkin 2014). Ashby's
ultrastability is about staying viable when conditions shift. v1 had a static environment where random switching was already at
ceiling.
Environment: T = 600 steps; hunger H and fatigue F start at 0.3. Local stock s starts U(0.3,1). The hunger rate is
r_t = 0.03 + 0.015·sin(2πt/150 + φ) + a random walk (σ = 0.002, clamped to [0.01, 0.06]), with φ ~ U(0, 2π).
Actions: eat: H −= 0.12·s, s −= 0.06 (min 0), F += 0.02. rest: F −= 0.07. move: s ~ U(0.2, 1), F += 0.05.
Every step H += r_t, F += 0.005, and s += 0.003 (capped at 1). Both H and F are clamped to be >= 0.
The agent observes H, F and s (not r_t).
Metric: fraction of steps with H < 1 AND F < 1 (there is no death; recovery counts).
Controls: fixed goal = always eat; random goal switching = a uniformly random action each step.

## V2-21: differentiation that independent noise cannot max out (replaces v1 CQM-21's entropy comparison)
Why: plain state entropy is maximal for independent fair coins, which is the v1 failure. Tononi, Sporns & Edelman (1994, PNAS)
neural complexity C_N is 0 for fully independent and for fully integrated systems, and high only when a system is both
integrated and differentiated. Tononi & Edelman (1998, Science) and perturbational complexity (Casali et al. 2013) likewise
require differentiated responses that are also informative.
System: the creature's 6-unit stochastic binary network (Glauber dynamics, β = 2, gain fixed). It is driven by one of 8 stimuli
(3-bit patterns, each bit adds ±1.0 to units 0, 2 and 4) for 2000 steps after a 200-step burn-in. The stimulus changes every 25 steps.
Measures, with plug-in entropies in bits:
 (a) C_N = Σ_{k=1}^{3} ( ⟨H(X_k^j)⟩_j − (k/6)·H(X) ), averaged over all subsets of size k.
 (b) I(S; X) = H(X) − H(X|S), the stimulus-conditioned repertoire differentiation.
Controls: uncoupled = the same units, inputs, biases and noise with all couplings set to 0; random coupled = weights i.i.d.
N(0, 1.5²) on the same 60% density mask, with no learning.
Comparisons: C_N vs uncoupled, C_N vs random coupled, I(S;X) vs random coupled.
