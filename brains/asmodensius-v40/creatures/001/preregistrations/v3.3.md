# Preregistration v3.3: spiking liquid time-constant (LTC) neurons vs fixed-tau LIF in the organism

Status: **PLAN, committed before any LTC test was run** (2026-10-29). The `ltc` option exists in organism.ts (off by default). It was smoke-tested only for ledger and determinism. No task metric has been looked at.

## Sources (the mechanism is adapted from these, not reproduced)
- Hasani, Lechner, Amini, Rus, Grosu. *Liquid Time-constant Networks.* AAAI 2021 (arXiv:2006.04439). dx/dt = -[1/τ + f(x,I)]·x + f(x,I)·A, so the effective τ depends on the input.
- Hasani et al. *Closed-form continuous-time neural networks (CfC).* Nature Machine Intelligence 4, 2022. A closed-form gate approximates the LTC ODE. It is used here only as the justification for an exp(-dt/τ(I)) per-substep update.
- Yin, Corradi, Bohté. *Accurate online training of dynamical spiking neural networks through Forward Propagation Through Time.* Nature Machine Intelligence 5, 2023. This paper introduces the LTC spiking neuron (LTC-SN), with input-driven learned time constants.
- Vanhalle & Gielen. *Spiking Liquid Time-Constant Networks (sLTC).* IEEE ICECS 2025.

**Difference from these papers (honest):** the τ gates here are hand-set biophysical gates, not learned by gradient. The rules are:
- τ_i = s · τ0 · (1 + a·ACh) / ((1 + b·|I_syn,i|)(1 + c·NE)), with τ0 = 1/ln 1.25, so the closed-gate decay of 0.8 equals the fixed LIF.
- The ACh gate applies to CTX and HIP. The NE gate applies to CTX.
- The input is scaled by (1−decay)/0.2, so the steady-state gain matches the fixed-τ LIF.

This is an "LTC-inspired spiking neuron". It is not a trained LTC network.

## Arms
- `full` = organism with `ltc: true`.
- `control` = the identical organism with fixed τ (the v3.1/v3.2 code).

Same seeds, 16 per arm, test seeds 1000..1015. Constants a, b, c may be tuned on seeds 2000..2015 only. The pass rule is d ≥ 0.8 and t ≥ 2.75, LTC better than fixed. If fixed τ is significantly better (d ≤ -0.8, t ≤ -2.75), the result is recorded as "LTC hurts".

## Items
- **L1 memory-guided choice:** the V3-C2 protocol metric (probe EV of trained sites, day 4 08:00), LTC vs fixed.
- **L2 sleep effect under LTC:** the V3-C2 contrast (sleep minus forced wake EV), compared between architectures.
- **L3 level benefit:** the V3-C8b metric (probe EV at level 4 minus level 1, same state), LTC vs fixed.
- **L4 delayed recall:** the RPT-1 protocol accuracy (v3.1), LTC vs fixed.
- **L5 robustness to timescale shift:** the L1 metric at tauScale ∈ {0.5, 2} minus at tauScale 1, mean of the two (less negative = more robust), LTC vs fixed.
