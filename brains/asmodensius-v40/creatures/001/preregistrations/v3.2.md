# Preregistration v3.2: mechanism improvements for V3-C2 and V3-C8b

Status: **PLAN, committed before code** (2026-10-29). The v3.0 failures stay on record (results/organism_v30.json, commit 52b3e8b):
C2 failed vs replay-off (t 2.63), and C8b failed (d 0.22).
The protocols, metrics, seeds and pass rule are identical to v3.0. Only mechanisms change. The full v3.0 suite (C1..C11) is re-run on the new code as a regression check under the same rule.

## Diagnosis (from v3.0 code)
- **C8b:** the spiking-race accumulator leaks 40% per substep (v = 0.6 v + ...). Its effective memory is about 2.5 substeps, so more substeps at a higher level average out almost no noise. The level buys cost (C8a) but no integration.
- **C2:** NREM replay samples episodes uniformly, and awake learning already captures much of the value. Replay adds little beyond the sleep effect on adenosine noise.

## Planned mechanisms
1. **Level-dependent integration time constant.** The striatal accumulator leak falls with level (a longer integration window when more ATP is spent).
2. **Level-dependent deliberation.** At levels ≥ 3 the race adds the cortical feature-model prediction (model-based prior) to each site's drive. Planning depth in the navigation module = 2 × level.
3. **Prioritised replay** (Mattar & Daw 2018): NREM samples episodes in proportion to |RPE at storage| + ε. Ripple-locked replay bursts are gated by N2/N3 spindles.
4. **Synaptic homeostasis** (Tononi & Cirelli 2014): N3 down-scales plastic cortical synapses toward baseline. This is reported in the inspector and has no test of its own.
Constants are tuned on seeds 2000..2015 only. A frozen addendum is appended before the test-seed run.

### §F frozen addendum (2026-10-29, before the test-seed run)
Implemented as planned:
- Level-dependent leak: keep = 1 − 0.4/level, with input × (1−keep)/0.4.
- Feature-model prior at level ≥ 3.
- Prioritised NREM replay ∝ |RPE| + 0.05, with replayed priority × 0.9.
- N3 down-scaling 0.0005·(0.35 − w).

Planning depth in the maze module is 3 × level, not the planned 2 × level. Spindle-gated ripple bursts were NOT implemented.
