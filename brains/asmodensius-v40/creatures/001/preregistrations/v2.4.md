# Preregistration v2.4: 11 more roadmap capability tests

Frozen 2026-10-29 (Asia/Manila). This file was committed before any v2.4 test or mechanism code was written or run.
v1, v2, v2.1, v2.2 and v2.3 are unchanged.

## Shared rules
- 16 seeds, 1000..1015. Seed s draws from the stream mulberry(s*1009 + k), where k is the test's salt listed below.
- Pass = Cohen's d >= 0.8 AND Welch t >= 2.75 against every listed control, using the frozen v1 `compare()` (zero variance gives infinity, as in v2.1). Every "Additional" requirement must also hold.
- The test code (world, metric, controls) lives in the frozen `v24Tests()`. Mechanisms live outside it, behind the interfaces named below. Only mechanisms may change, and each change is logged in HARNESS_CHANGES.md.
- These are small harness models of each capability, not demonstrations in the rendered creature. The site must say so.

## RM-9 Homeostatic regulation (salt 9)
T_ext(t) = 2 sin(2 pi t/500 + phi) + 0.002 t + N(0, 0.1^2), with phi ~ U(0, 2 pi).
x_{t+1} = x_t + 0.1 (T_ext - x_t) + clip(u_t, -0.3, 0.3), starting from x_0 = 0, over 1000 steps. The controller sees only x_t and its own past u.
Metric: fraction of steps with |x| < 0.5. Control: open loop (u = 0).
Additional: the full mean is at least 2x the control mean.

## RM-29 Lifelong learning without catastrophic forgetting (salt 29)
x ~ N(0, I_8), task A target = wA.x[0..5], task B target = wB.x[2..7], with wA, wB ~ N(0, I_6).
The learner sees (x, task id, target). It trains for 400 steps on A and then 400 steps on B, one sample per step.
Retention = R^2 on A (500 fresh samples) after B.
Metric: retention. Control: plain SGD on one shared linear model (lr 0.05) that ignores task id.
Additional: full mean retention >= 0.8, and full R^2 on B >= 0.8.

## RM-36 Neuromodulated plasticity (salt 36)
Four Bernoulli arms with p = {0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8}, shuffled per seed. 300 trials, softmax choice over w with beta = 3.
The mechanism supplies the gating signal g(r) used in w_a += 0.1 g(r).
Metric: mean reward over trials 200..299. Control: ungated Hebbian, g = 1.

## RM-42 Delayed gratification (salt 42)
200 trials. On each trial: energy E ~ U(0.2, 2), delay D ~ U{2..10}. Option "small now" gives 1. Option "large later" gives 3 if E > 0.1 D, otherwise 0.
The agent sees E and D.
Metric: mean payoff. Control: myopic (always small now).
Additional: the full agent picks large-later on >= 50% of trials.

## RM-53 Chemotaxis (salt 53)
Goal at the origin. Start at distance 50, at a random angle. Concentration c = exp(-d/20) * (1 + N(0, 0.01^2)).
Each step: move 1 unit along the heading, then the agent returns a tumble probability from dc = c_t - c_{t-1}. On a tumble, the new heading is ~ U(0, 2 pi). 300 steps.
Metric: -final distance.
Controls: (a) random walk (tumble every step); (b) the ablated circuit (the same agent called with dc = 0).

## RM-73 Hunger-driven risk sensitivity (salt 73)
400 trials. Energy is drawn E ~ U(0, 2) on each trial. Safe gives 0.5. Risky gives 1.5 with p = 1/3, otherwise 0. The agent sees E.
Metric: P(risky | E < 0.5) - P(risky | E > 1.5). Control: fixed policy, P(risky) = 0.5.
Additional: the full mean P(risky | low) > 0.5 > the full mean P(risky | high), i.e. a sign flip.

## RM-78 Numerosity, Weber law (salt 78)
n in {4, 8, 16, 32}, 50 trials each. The estimator returns n_hat.
Metric: least-squares slope of log sd(n_hat) against log n.
Control: flat noise, n_hat = n + N(0, 2^2).
Additional: the full mean slope is in [0.7, 1.3].

## RM-82 Extinction and spontaneous recovery (salt 82)
US magnitude m ~ U(0.8, 1.2). Protocol: 50 CS+US trials, then 50 CS-alone trials, then wait(100), then 1 test trial.
Interface: trial(us), wait(n), resp().
Metric: resp(test) - resp(end of extinction).
Control: Rescorla-Wagner, alpha = 0.1 (unlearning).
Additional: the full response at the end of extinction is < 0.3 m.

## RM-96 Radiation-tolerant genome repair (salt 96)
256-bit genome. The mechanism encodes it into <= 768 stored bits. Then, for 100 steps: every stored bit flips with p = 0.01 (10x the base dose of 0.001), and repair(store) is called.
Metric: the fraction of decode(store) bits that equal the genome. Control: no repair (identity encode and decode).
Additional: full >= 0.95 in >= 15/16 seeds.

## RM-97 Dormancy / cryptobiosis (salt 97)
E_0 = 1. Food gives 0.01 per step, except that there is no food from t0 ~ U{150..250} for 1500 steps. 2000 steps in total.
Active metabolism is 0.002 per step. A dormant state costs 0.0002 per step. The agent sees only food presence and E.
Death occurs at E <= 0.
Metric: steps alive. Control: no dormancy.
Additional: the full agent is alive and active at t = 2000 in >= 15/16 seeds.

## RM-52 Graceful degradation under neuron loss (salt 52)
s ~ U(-1, 1). The mechanism encodes s into 64 unit activities and decodes from the surviving units. k random units are silenced, for k in {0, 8, ..., 56}. 200 samples per k.
P(k) = 1 - MSE / Var(s).
Metric: -max_k (P(k) - P(k+8)).
Control: a non-redundant code (unit 0 = s, every other unit 0; decode = unit 0).
Additional: the full mean P(32) >= 0.5.
