## A. Energy budget (top priority)
Diagnosis (dev seeds): v3.7 eats ~37 kJ/day in standalone runs but spends ~77 kJ/day as heat; fat falls ~35/day. In the lab dome with ample food, 9/10 creatures died, median 17.3 days (seeds 2000–2001); lab/viability.ts on the default dome gave a median of 11.2 days. The cause is not a food shortage. The creature never increases foraging or reduces expenditure as its fat falls.
Mechanisms (all gated to v38):
1. Leptin/AgRP-like forage drive. fatDef = clamp(1 − fat/fSet); forage probability += fK·fatDef. Falling leptin disinhibits arcuate AgRP neurons, which increases food seeking.
2. Adaptive thermogenesis (fasting metabolic down-regulation). Basal brain, body and heart costs are scaled by thm = max(thMin, 1 − thK·fatDef). This matches the fall in energy expenditure seen in fasting and caloric restriction (Leibel 1995; Minnesota study), with a floor of 0.6.
3. Muscle accretion saturates at mMax; surplus amino acids are deaminated (0.75 to glucose, 0.1 to urea, 0.15 to heat). Without this, protein built muscle without limit.
Dev evidence: ample food, 40 days, seeds 2000–2005: 0/30 deaths, fat ~355–385, intake ~81–88 kJ/day. Standalone runs: fat stable ~335–340, muscle ~170.

## A2. Fasting physiology (v3.8a, gated by `fast`; most terms act only when liver glycogen is below 5)
1. Glycogen-gated PEPCK induction: amino-acid gluconeogenesis is multiplied by (1 + gngK·g), where g = clamp(1 − glycogen/5).
2. Glucocorticoid-driven muscle proteolysis is multiplied by (1 + protK·g). This is phase II of starvation.
3. CPT1 disinhibition: hepatic ketogenesis is multiplied by (1 + ketK·g).
4. The brain oxidises ketones before glucose (substrate competition; Owen 1967).
5. Renal ketone reabsorption: excretion falls from 0.003/min to 0.0008/min.
6. FFA product inhibition of lipolysis: lipolysis is multiplied by clamp(1 − FFA/6).
Dev evidence (fast.ts; seeds 2000–2005): death at 6.6–6.7 days, with fat about 72–76 and muscle about 40 (from about 125) at death. Stages on every seed, in days: foraging up 0.5, glycogen <20% 0.93, ketone >1 at 1.9–2.0, torpor (daily expenditure <85% of fed) at 2–3.

## E. Viability tests (v3.8a; replaces the "≥30 days" criterion). Script viability_v38.ts; evaluator viab_eval_v38.py (frozen).
- **VIAB-a, steady state with ample food.** Lab dome, ample arm, 12 days, seeds 1000–1005 (30 creatures). Slopes are OLS slopes of daily fat and liver glycogen over days 7–12. Pass requires all of:
  - 0 deaths;
  - fat slope: the 95% CI of the mean includes 0 or lies above it (upper bound ≥ 0), and the lower bound is > −2 per day (equivalence margin, 0.5% of fSet per day);
  - glycogen slope: upper bound ≥ 0 and lower bound > −0.5 per day;
  - mean |intake − expenditure| / expenditure < 0.10 over days 7–12.
  - Dev (2000–2005): fat slope 0.19 [−0.11, 0.49], glycogen 0.03 [−0.06, 0.12], imbalance 0.026, 0 deaths.
- **VIAB-b, starvation.** Standalone organism, one fed day, then no food; seeds 1000–1015.
  - The reserve-scaled window is R = (fat0 + glycogen0 + 0.5·muscle0) / fed-day expenditure.
  - A seed is OK if all stage times exist and are ordered: forage ≤ min(ketosis, torpor), glycogen ≤ ketosis, max(ketosis, torpor) < death. Death must also fall within [0.5R, 1.5R].
  - Pass if ≥ 15/16 seeds are OK. Dev: 6/6.
- **VIAB-c, recovery.** Same as VIAB-b, but food is restored at day 3 (after ketosis, before death) and the run lasts 8 days. A seed recovers if it is alive at day 8, glycogen ≥ 0.5·glycogen0, ketones < 0.5, and it has regained at least half the fat it lost.
  - Pass if ≥ 15/16 seeds recover.
  - Control: the no-refeed arm on the same seeds. Lifespan is censored at 8 days; d/t are reported.
  - Dev: 6/6. On dev, an earlier "fat slope ≥ 0 after day 6" rule was replaced by the regain rule because it failed on a recovered plateau. This change was made on dev seeds, before the freeze.
- Durations (12 days, about 7 days, 8 days) are the shortest that cover the transient plus a 6-day slope window, the full starvation course, and a refeed plateau.
- Caveat: the terminal event is still glucose failure (blood glucose < 0.9 for 2 h) with about 20% of fat left. Phase II proteolysis supplies the gluconeogenesis until muscle runs down.

## C. efe conflict (thaler9 vs AINF)
Mechanism: choose() is a spiking race. A large, uniform epistemic bonus saturates the race and erases learned site-value differences in the novel probe (thaler9 ≈ 0.05).
Fix: reference-dependent (range-adapted) epistemic value. The bonus is computed as (IG − efeC·IG_ref), with IG_ref = min IG over accessible sites. This follows adaptive range coding of value in OFC (Padoa-Schioppa 2009; Louie & Glimcher divisive normalisation). It is not dream-gated: the efe term acts only in waking choice(), so "exploration drops in dreams" would be vacuous.
