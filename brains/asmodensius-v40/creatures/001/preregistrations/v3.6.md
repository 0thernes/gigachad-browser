# Preregistration v3.6: steering API, debiased noise, new mechanisms for failing items, lab-handoff mechanisms, WASM backends

Status: written during Round 17 tuning (seeds 2000..2015 only). It is **committed together with the frozen v3.6 code BEFORE any run on test seeds 1000..1015**. As in v3.5, the mechanism work started on tuning seeds before this text was written; no test-seed data existed when it was written. All earlier results (v1..v3.5) stay on record unchanged.

## 0. Diagnosis (tuning seeds only, v3.5 organism)
- **RPT-2.** CTX modules 0/1 encode a and b almost perfectly (decoders ~1.0/0.99), but XOR decodes at 0.53. Cells are linear in (a, b); there is no conjunctive nonlinearity.
- **HOT-3.** Low-confidence bins have accuracy 0.26–0.36. Opting out pays 0.3 against ~0.25 chance, so the most opting out can gain is about 0.05 × (fraction opted out). Under the frozen test this item is structurally close to unpassable.
- **AE-1.** Per-period reward rate falls from ~0.4 to ~0.3 after every reversal, and the value map is relearned from scratch each time.
- **CQM12 / thaler9.** The NREM factor model (featC + featS) fitted to single episodes does worse than the mean.
- **activeInference.** The epistemic bonus is a heuristic recency term, not an information gain.
- **AST-1.** A fixed-covariance Kalman filter. **CQM04.** Reach costs ATP, so selection favours low reach. This is test economics, so there is no genuine organism fix.
- **GWT-3, ctm, CQM08.** These sit on the ceiling (above 0.95 of max) with clean one-hot stimuli.
- **Lab handoff (lab/HANDOFF-R17-scorecard.md).**
  - CTX→THAL feedback is about 15% of THAL→CTX.
  - Cortical recurrence is net stabilising; there is no late evoked phase.
  - INS carries no glucose signal (corr −0.07), while HYP does (−0.77).

## A. Mechanisms (option `mind: "v36"`, the new default; `ORG_MIND=v35` reproduces v3.5 bit-for-bit, verified on 3 configurations × 4000 ticks)
Steering queue:
1. **Debiased decision-noise sign.** The Born-rule bit is von Neumann debiased: draw pairs, 01→0, 10→1, discard 00/11; after 8 discarded pairs, fall back to the seeded stream.
2. **Full-state `snapshot()` / `restore()`.** Every field is captured, including typed arrays, the Mind and every PRNG state. The snapshot is JSON-safe and can be restored into an organism with any seed.
3. **Light input for the dome.**
   - `setLight(x∈[0,1] | null)` and `setPhotoperiod(on, off)`.
   - Graded light scales SCN entrainment and melatonin suppression. Binary light is bit-identical to v3.5.
4. **NE term in the sleep drive.** `drive −= neK·max(0, NE−0.3)`, because LC noradrenaline inhibits VLPO sleep-active neurons in the sleep/wake flip-flop (Chou et al. 2002; Saper et al. 2005).

Failure-targeted mechanisms:

5. **HOT-1: learned top-down precision.** The blend weight is fitted by normalised LMS to the teaching signal.
6. **AST-1: adaptive Kalman.** Innovation covariance matching (Mehra 1972).
7. **AE-1: latent-cause inference.** Two value maps and a leaky log-likelihood ratio; when it crosses threshold, the maps swap (Gershman, Blei & Niv 2010).
8. **RPT-2 / CQM06: dendritic NMDA gating.** Mg-block gating of cross-module input (Larkum 2013).
9. **CQM12 / thaler9: schema extraction.** NREM weighted alternating least squares fits featC/featS to the hippocampal per-site outcome statistics, with shrinkage to 0.15.
10. **activeInference: expected information gain.** Mutual information between the next outcome and the site's reward probability under a forgetting Beta belief (Friston et al. 2015).

Lab handoff R17:

11. **L6-like CTX→THAL feedback.** A slow (NMDA-pathway) modulatory projection, kind 8, on its own RNG stream. Lesion: `ctFbLesion`.
12. **Slow cortical NMDA current.** Applied on within-module CTX→CTX assembly synapses, tau `slowTau` ticks. Lesion: `slowLesion`.
13. **Visceral glucose afferent to INS.** Opponent glucose-on / glucose-off cells, a vagal/NTS-like route. Lesion: `visceralLesion`.

## B. WASM backends (opt-in; organism/backends.ts)
- **Moonlab.** The Moonlab statevector runs the organism's exact circuit. Only the Born probabilities come from Moonlab; all draws stay on the seeded stream, so runs remain deterministic.
- **Eshkol.** The Eshkol VM evaluates the sleep/wake flip-flop rule as a Scheme program every tick.
- Both are attached with `setQuantumBackend` / `setRuleEngine`. They are not used in the scored suite.

## C. Items
1. **Unchanged suite.** The v3.1 suite, L1..L5 and K1/K2, with the same pass rule: d ≥ 0.8 and t ≥ 2.75 vs every control, plus the non-ceiling rule. Tests are unchanged.
2. **New tests (tests_v36.ts).**
   - R-variants of the three ceiling items, using the HOT-2 degraded stimulus. Same rule, including the ceiling rule. The originals stay scored as before.
     - **GWT-3R**: vs broadcast lesioned.
     - **ctm-R**: vs broadcast lesioned.
     - **CQM08R**: vs replay lesioned.
   - **SNAP** (tolerance test): restore into a different-seed organism after a JSON round trip; the run must be identical on 16/16 seeds.
   - **NOISESIGN**: on a fixed, strongly biased state, |P(+)−0.5| < 0.05.
   - **LIGHT**: dark-dome minus lit-dome melatonin at midnight > 0.
   - **INSGLC** (handoff acceptance): INS balanced accuracy ≥ 0.75, and d/t pass vs visceral-cut and shuffled labels.
   - **RPTLATE** (handoff acceptance): late evoked CTX > 0, and cutting CTX→THAL or CTX→CTX lowers late by ≥ 50% while early changes by < 20%.
3. **WASM equivalence** (results/wasm_equiv_v36.json). Pass requires all of:
   - Moonlab vs TS probabilities within 1e-12 on 5000 random circuits.
   - 0 Eshkol rule mismatches in 2000 cases.
   - Full-state identical after 2 days with both backends on seeds 1000..1003.

## D. Honesty constraints
- No frozen tests are edited. R-variants are new items reported next to the originals and do not replace them.
- HOT-3 and CQM04 get no dedicated fix (see §0). They are reported as measured.
- Mechanisms that hurt on tuning seeds ship switched off; they are listed in §F.
