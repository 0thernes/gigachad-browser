# Preregistration v3.0: coupling tests on the integrated organism (creatures/001/organism)

Frozen 2026-10-29 (Europe/London). This file and the mechanism and test code (organism/organism.ts, organism/tests.ts, organism/run.ts)
are committed together **before** the first run on test seeds 1000..1015. Mechanism constants were tuned only on seeds 2000..2015.
The tuning output is kept at results/organism_v30_tuning_seeds2000.txt. It includes C8b, which FAILED on tuning seeds and is preregistered unchanged.
v1..v2.7 are unchanged.

**Pass rule** (frozen harness compare()): Cohen's d >= 0.8 and Welch t >= 2.75 vs EVERY control, n = 16 seeds per arm, test seeds 1000..1015.
**Exceptions, stated in advance:**
- C9 is an equivalence test. It passes if |d| < 0.5.
- C10 and C11 are tolerance tests. Their criteria are stated below; d and t do not apply.

The system under test is the organism module itself. In node, the 100M GPU network is not run (its only input, predErr→LC, is 0).
Protocol times are in simulated minutes, with t = 0 at midnight of day 1. A "probe" is 80 choices with learning frozen.
The probe's metric is mean true reward probability of the chosen sites (expected reward), or choice entropy where stated.

| ID | Claim | Metric | Full | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1a | Starvation lowers tonic DA | mean extracellular DA over awake ticks of day 3 | fed | starved (food never delivered) |
| C1b | Starvation lowers exploration | probe choice entropy at day 4 08:00 on a standardised value map (w = 0.1 + 0.6 p) | fed | starved |
| C1c | DA mediates C1b | same as C1b | starved + DA clamped at 0.25 | starved, DA free |
| C2 | Sleep deprivation degrades memory-guided choice | probe expected reward, trained sites, day 4 08:00 | normal | forced wake all days; sleep with replay disabled |
| C3 | Dreams (REM recombination) improve later choice among novel sites | probe expected reward restricted to the 8 never-visited sites, day 4 08:00 | full replay | no replay; NREM only; REM with shuffled content |
| C4 | Gut-brain axis lesion changes behaviour | forage attempts in 2 h before minus 2 h after a 20 kJ meal at day 2 11:00 (no pellets in window) | intact | subdiaphragmatic vagotomy |
| C5 | LC stimulation raises HR via the sympathetic chain | mean HR in 30 min of LC stimulation minus the preceding hour (day 2 14:00) | intact + stim | sham; sympathectomy + stim |
| C6 | SCN drives the sleep-wake rhythm | night (22-04) minus day (10-18) sleep fraction, days 2-4 | intact | SCN lesion |
| C7 | Thought curvature is felt | LC rate at t+1 for kappa(t) above vs below the median (awake, day 2 07:00-21:00) | intact | insula lesion; insula fed time-shuffled kappa |
| C8a | Intelligence level drains energy | mean brain ATP demand, awake, day 2 | level 4 forced | level 1 forced |
| C8b | Intelligence level improves choice | probe expected reward at day 4 08:00, level switched one tick before the probe in otherwise identical twins | level 4 | level 1 |
| C9 | Quantum source gives no advantage (equivalence) | probe expected reward, day 4 | quantum (Born-rule 3-qubit) | classical coin |
| C10 | Energy conservation | max relative ledger error over every run in the suite | must be < 1e-9 | the deliberately leaky ledger must give > 1e-3 |
| C11 | Determinism | state hash after 600 ticks | same seed must give identical hashes | different seeds must differ |

**Known in advance (not hidden):**
- C5, C6 and C7 test wiring that is directly coded (stim → LC → symp → HR; SCN → process C; kappa → INS/LC). They show the coupling is live and lesionable; they are not discoveries.
- C1-C3 involve longer causal chains (diet → amino acids/fuel → VTA → DA kinetics → spiking-race noise → choice; sleep stage → replay → synapses → choice).
- On tuning seeds, forcing level 4 for 3 days *lowered* probe reward (d -0.43). The likely cause is higher adenosine from the extra ATP turnover, a real cost/benefit trade-off. It is reported, not tested.
