## B2. AE-1 diagnosis and fix (2026-10-31, dev only)
- **Diagnosis.** The RPE lesion did change wSite learning, but the lesion arm stayed at about 0.30 in every variant. The weak arm was the *full* creature.
  - Its mK blend pulls value toward the Beta posterior mean.
  - That posterior counts outcomes forever, so after each reversal it is confidently stale and drags value back.
  - mK = 0 raised full AE-1 (d 1.15), and the efe lesion raised it too.
  - Affect attitudes and "wanting" were also reward-driven value caches that bypassed the RPE system.
- **Fixes (mechanistic):**
  - (1) **volLeak:** Beta beliefs leak toward the prior at a rate proportional to the volatility learned from unsigned RPEs (Behrens et al. 2007). The RPE lesion therefore also removes adaptive forgetting.
  - (2) **Affect gating:** attitude learning and incentive-salience growth are gated by the phasic RPE (Berridge & Robinson 1998). An RPE lesion now removes them too.
  - A latent-cause swap of beliefs was tried and rejected (d 0.58).
- **Tuning on 2032–2047 (volLeak 0.002 / 0.005 / 0.01 → AE-1 d 1.10 / 0.74 / 1.37; noisy, not monotonic):** chose 0.01. With it:

## B21 (DRAFT; chosen 2026-11-01 06:58 UTC, before any run). Dendritic binding and the action-outcome model
Seeds:
- dev 3285-3300 (isolation and creature);
- held-out 3301-3316 (run once, after the design is frozen);
- AE1 pool 3317-3364 (48 seeds);
- foraging check: v5.3 vs v5.4 viability on 3189 and 3205 (existing blocks), plus dev 3285.
Mechanisms:
- DMUL: two-compartment cells. The basal drive is the cell's own module; the apical drive comes from another module through fixed random weights. Output = basal x sigmoid(apical gain), a multiplicative coincidence (Larkum 1999/2013).
  - Isolation: linear XOR decode from DMUL units vs additive units (same weights) vs apical cut.
  - In the creature, cut by mixLesion.
- AOM: per-site action-outcome model. It learns each site's base yield (Kalman, random-walk drift) and a shared learned consequence curve: yield ratio vs time since own last success, in 8 bins, NLMS.
  - Predicted reward = base x curve.
  - Information gain = 0.5 log(1 + predictive variance / noise), added to site drive. It is part of the epistemic term, so efeLesion removes it.
  - Isolation: a depleting 16-site bandit, IG vs no-IG vs no-consequence-model.
Pass rule unchanged.
