# Addendum v2.7a: mechanism changes made after tuning-seed runs, before any test-seed run

Frozen 2026-10-29 (Europe/London). Nothing in v2.7 has been run on test seeds 1000..1015. Tests, metrics, controls and pass rules are unchanged.
Tuning-seed runs (2000..2015, harness/tune27.ts) showed two mechanisms that could not express the capability:

1. CAP-C: with fixed weights 0.02*count, every cell saturated at 1. The readout was exactly 0 for every trial and AUROC 0.475 for both
   the real and the shuffled connectome. Change (mechanism): synaptic scaling. Each cell's chemical input is divided by its total
   incoming chemical synapse count (gain 1); gap-junction input is divided by its total incoming gap count (gain 0.5).
   One structural change was tried. Deviation note: v2.7 said "no parameter may be tuned". This structural fix replaces that, and
   it is disclosed here. On tuning seeds the real connectome separates anterior from posterior touch perfectly, but in the REVERSED
   direction (AUROC 0.00: anterior drives AVB > AVA) under the Dale/GABA sign rule. No sign was changed to fix the direction.
   The test is run as preregistered and is expected to FAIL its direction requirement.
2. CAP-S: the v2.6 planner treats unknown edges as open, so the transitions the observer saw made no difference (d 0.00).
   Change: a new uncertainty-aware planner `seekPref` (Dijkstra; known-open edges cost 1, unknown edges cost UNK, walls impassable;
   same replanning and belief revision as v2.6). UNK tried on tuning seeds: 3 (d 0.43), 6 (0.83), 20 (1.01), 100 (1.01). UNK = 20.
   v2.6 RM-83 keeps the v2.6 planner, so its results do not change.
v2.7 text "seeks the goal with the v2.6 planner" is superseded by this addendum for CAP-S only.
