Bible — constitutive claims of this paper (as publicly described)

  • Trained nets under progressive damage exhibit structured, non-random residual behavior.
  • Phantom / virtual-input outputs appear in advanced damage regimes.
  • The progression can be analogized to near-death / hallucinatory phenomenology.
  • These dynamics are a scientific window into creative generation from associative memory.

Not claimed here by this corpus: that the net is phenomenally conscious.
