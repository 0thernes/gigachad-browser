## A–D

- **Active Inference (`FEP` / `EFE`):** Karl Friston's variational principle whereby a biological or synthetic organism minimizes Variational Free Energy $F$ (perceptual surprise) and Expected Free Energy $G(\pi)$ (epistemic exploration + pragmatic homeostasis).
- **Atom of Thought (`AoT`):** An irreducible 64-D continuous latent proposition $a_k \in \mathbb{R}^{64}$ paired with a 1,024-bit `BigUint64Array(16)` Hyperdimensional Computing (`HDC`) signature.
- **Bekenstein-Hawking Entropy ($S_{\text{BH}}$):** Black hole horizon entropy $S_{\text{BH}} = \frac{k_B c^3 A}{4 G \hbar} = \pi (r_+^2 + a^2)$ in geometric units, computed in `KerrNewmanBlackHoleBekensteinPageBrain`.
- **BitNet `b1.58` Ternary Quantization:** 2-bit weight packing (`Uint8Array` Tier 6, 4 synapses per byte) constraining weights to $\{-1, 0, +1\}$ for multiplication-free inner products.
- **Corecursive Anamorphism ($\text{ana}_f$):** Coinductive stream/tree generator unfolding future sensorimotor trajectories from an initial seed state without requiring termination.
- **Cusp Catastrophe ($\Xi$):** Thom-Zeeman potential surface $V(\Xi) = \Xi^4 + \alpha \Xi^2 + \beta \Xi$ whose gradient flow $\dot{\Xi} = -(4\Xi^3 + 2\alpha\Xi + \beta)$ governs abrupt regime shifts between divergent ideation ($\alpha < 0$) and convergent focus ($\alpha > 0$).
- **Dale's Law (`DaleConv`):** Neurobiological constraint requiring each neuron's outgoing synapses to be exclusively excitatory ($+1$, 80%) or exclusively inhibitory ($-1$, 20%): $W_{\text{eff}} = |W_{\text{raw}}| \odot \text{sign}$.
- **Decursive Catamorphism / Deduplication ($\text{cata}_g$):** Algebra folding and deduplicating cyclic or redundant thought branches in the Graph of Thought (`GoT`) via 1,024-bit XOR Hamming distance and Tarjan SCC condensation.
